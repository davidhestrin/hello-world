# Hello World - Location Based Greeting

A dynamic Hello World app that greets visitors based on their IP-detected location with local flair!

## Features

- **IP Geolocation**: Automatically detects your location using your IP address
- **Localized Greetings**: Says hello in 40+ languages based on your country
- **Country Flags**: Displays your country's flag emoji
- **Fun Facts**: Shows interesting facts about your country
- **Time-Aware Theming**: Background changes based on your local time of day (morning, afternoon, evening, night)
- **Live Clock**: Shows your current local time with timezone
- **Responsive Design**: Looks great on desktop and mobile

## How It Works

1. When you open the page, it fetches your location from your IP address
2. Based on your country, it selects a localized greeting in your language
3. A random fun fact about your country is displayed
4. The theme adapts to your local time of day

## Supported Countries

Includes localized greetings for: USA, UK, France, Germany, Spain, Italy, Japan, China, Korea, Brazil, Australia, India, Mexico, Canada, and 30+ more countries!

## Usage

Simply open `index.html` in a web browser. No server required - it's a pure client-side application.

## Tech Stack

- HTML5
- CSS3 (with animations and responsive design)
- Vanilla JavaScript
- ip-api.com for geolocation
